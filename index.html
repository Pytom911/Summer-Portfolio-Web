About me

Hi, I’m PytomDev, a web developer who loves when things run calm and fast. I turn rough ideas into polished interfaces that are easy to use, easy to maintain and kind to every screen.

My days follow the tide: sketch in the morning, build through the afternoon, ship before sunset. I care about clean code, readable design and pages that load before your coffee cools. Currently exploring the shoreline between product design and web performance.
